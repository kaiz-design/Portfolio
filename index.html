How I work

I prioritize a user-centered approach in my work

Design to me is a process of digging deep to identify real user problems and solve them in a logical yet creative way. Empathy is the key to design product with seamless user experience. My design decisions are informed by user needs and complex limitation. In my work, I consistently strive to balance business goals with user needs. I believe that a positive user experience is essential for both the success of the company and the growth of its products.

My superpower lies in my flexibility and adaptability

I am very flexible at work or at home, and I can quickly adapt to new environment. This superpower allows me to respond effectively to changing priorities and unexpected challenges. It also makes me a strong team players. My ability to pivot quickly enables me to find innovative solutions that meet both team and project needs. I actively seek feedback and engage in open communication, ensuring that I align with my colleagues and stakeholders.

Constraint is my powerful source of creativity

I work with constraints everyday, and a lot of the time, being a product designer in Design System, I create constraints too. The constraints can be time, resources, or guidelines, and they forced me to explore new ideas and approaches in my day to day work. One of the constraint I embrace and advocate for is accessibility! I believe in the power of inclusive design and the importance of making digital experience accessible to all.
